## Adapters

- Konstruktor `sqlite3.Connection` injection (konzistentan sa ACS-F1-005).
- `save_*` idempotentni (`INSERT ... ON CONFLICT(id) DO UPDATE` + join-table
  `DELETE`/re-`INSERT` za claims/items).
- Enum-ovi: save `enum.value`, get `Enum(value)` (vraćeni kao domain enum, ne
  str). Timestamp-ovi `isoformat()`/`fromisoformat()`. Typed ID `NewType(row[...])`.
- `SqliteContentRepository.list_campaign_content(campaign_id)` kroz
  `content_pieces JOIN campaign_items JOIN campaign_plans WHERE campaign_id`.
