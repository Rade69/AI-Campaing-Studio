## Implementation summary

1. **`MaterializePerformanceSnapshots`** (nov use-case):
   - za svaki `PerformanceImportRow` sa `match_status == "MATCHED"` i
     `errors == ()` kreira JEDAN `PerformanceSnapshot`;
   - MATCHED + invalid (errors non-empty) → `skipped_invalid_count`,
     nikad ne ruši batch; UNMATCHED/AMBIGUOUS/None → ignorisani;
   - mapiranje `mapped_values` → `CanonicalMetricSet` sa ISTOM
     int/float podjelom kao `row_parsing.py` (6 int: reach/impressions/
     engagements/clicks/conversions/video_views; 3 float: spend/revenue/
     watch_time_seconds); `int(float(...))` za int polja (toleriše
     "100.0"); prazne ćelije → `None`;
   - `period` = `MetricPeriod` iz `period_start`/`period_end`;
     `observed_at` = `batch.imported_at` (dokumentovan izbor), fallback
     `period.end`;
   - `raw_metrics = row.raw_values` (audit trail, isti princip kao
     `PerformanceImportRow`);
   - **idempotencija (dokumentovana odluka)**: deterministički
     `PerformanceSnapshotId(f"snap-{row.id}")` + postojeći UPSERT
     (`ON CONFLICT(id) DO UPDATE`) u `SqlitePerformanceRepository` —
     drugi poziv prepisuje isti red, ne duplira (test dokazuje COUNT==1).
2. **Bridge** — `confirm_performance_import` poslije `MatchPerformanceImportBatch`
   dodaje `MaterializePerformanceSnapshots(repo).execute(batch.id)` i vraća
   `materialized_count`/`skipped_invalid_count`. `MatchPerformanceImportBatch`
   NETAKNUT (nije u `allowed_paths`).
3. **DTO** — `ConfirmPerformanceImportResultUiModel` + 2 polja.
